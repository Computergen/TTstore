Cookie Notice

This Cookie Notice explains how TULA MARKET uses cookies, local storage, and similar tracking technologies on our marketplace.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your session, preferences, and cart activity.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and local storage to:

  • keep you signed in during your browsing session
  • remember items in your shopping cart
  • save your chosen display preferences
  • help maintain a secure and smooth marketplace experience

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: required for login, cart, and basic marketplace functionality.
  • Preference cookies: used to remember your settings and theme choice.
  • Analytics or performance cookies: used only when enabled to improve site performance and understand user behavior.

4. Your Choices

You may accept or decline cookies using the consent banner shown on our site. If you decline, some marketplace features may still work, but personalization and session persistence may be limited.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some services we use, such as payment processors or cloud hosting providers, may set cookies as part of their service. These are governed by their own privacy and cookie policies.

6. Contact

Questions about our cookies or privacy practices can be sent to tulastech@gmail.com.
