Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TULA MARKET collects, uses, protects, and shares personal information when you visit our marketplace, create an account, buy from a vendor, or sell through our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to operate the marketplace safely and efficiently. This may include:

  • Account information: full name, email address, password, profile picture, and role (customer or vendor).
  • Store information: business name, address, phone number, category, logo, banner, and store profile details for vendors.
  • Order information: shipping address, billing or payment reference details, items purchased, and order status.
  • Usage information: page visits, device information, browser details, and app activity used to improve the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account
  • process orders and complete deliveries
  • identify vendors and customers for transactions
  • operate seller dashboards and customer accounts
  • improve the experience, security, and reliability of TULA MARKET
  • send important account, order, or security updates

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary for:

  • authentication and account security
  • payment processing
  • image hosting and media delivery
  • customer support and order communication
  • legal compliance or prevention of fraud

4. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security. Use strong passwords and keep your account details private.

5. Cookies and Tracking

TULA MARKET may use browser cookies or local storage to remember sessions, preferences, and cart data. These tools help keep the shopping experience smooth and personalized.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You may also ask us to stop processing your personal information for certain purposes, subject to legal or contractual requirements.

7. Children’s Privacy

TULA MARKET is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent or legal basis.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated through the platform or by updating this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at tulastech@gmail.com.
