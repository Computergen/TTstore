Vendor Agreement

This Vendor Agreement sets the basic expectations for using TULA MARKET as a seller, brand, or local business on our platform.

1. Eligibility

To become a vendor, you must provide accurate business details, maintain a valid contact method, and act in good faith while selling through TULA MARKET.

2. Product Standards

Vendors must list products honestly, accurately describe items, and ensure their inventory is not misleading or counterfeit. TULA MARKET may remove listings that violate marketplace standards or customer trust.

3. Payment and Orders

Vendors agree to process and fulfill valid customer orders promptly, maintain a clear communication channel, and keep order information accurate and timely.

4. Customer Experience

Vendors are responsible for their store presentation, communication quality, and after-sales experience. Sellers must respect customer rights and act professionally in all transactions.

5. Brand and Content

Vendors are responsible for the content they publish, including logos, banners, descriptions, and product images. Content should not infringe on the rights of others or present false information.

6. Suspension or Removal

TULA MARKET may suspend, restrict, or remove vendor access if a store violates platform rules, breaks consumer trust, or fails to meet the expectations outlined in this agreement.

7. Limitations

TULA MARKET provides the marketplace technology and infrastructure, but vendors remain responsible for their products, pricing, shipping practices, and final customer service.

8. Contact

Questions about this agreement can be sent to tulastech@gmail.com.
